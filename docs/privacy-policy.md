# Privacy Policy

**Uptime Monitor**
Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Uptime Monitor ("the app") handles information. The app is developed by Ozan Arslan ("I", "me").

## Summary

Uptime Monitor does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your device, in a database that only the app can access:

- The services you add: names, URLs, request settings and keywords.
- The history of checks: timestamps, status codes, response times and error messages.
- Your app settings.

This data is never sent to me or to any third party. I have no servers that receive it and no way to access it.

## Network requests

To check a service, the app sends a request directly from your device to the URL you entered. Like any request on the internet, the server at that URL can see your device's IP address and the request details. Those servers are operated by whoever runs the service you are monitoring, not by me, and their own privacy policies apply.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local notifications when a service goes down or comes back up. These notifications are generated on your device. No push notification service or server is involved.

## No tracking, analytics or advertising

The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data. There are no user accounts.

## App stores

The App Store and Google Play may collect information such as crash reports or usage statistics if you have enabled this in your device settings. That collection is done by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

## Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time from **Settings → Delete all data** in the app, or by uninstalling the app. Since nothing is stored anywhere else, this removes your data completely.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
